# What the 27–28 July clipping work actually taught us

Two days spent on armour clipping. **Nothing shipped to the converter's output.** Three
candidate fixes died in validation, two population rankings turned out to be artifacts,
and four separate "results" turned out to be measurements of nothing.

That sounds like a bad outcome. It isn't the worst one — the worst one is shipping any
of those three fixes, and we came close to shipping two. What follows is written so the
next attempt starts from here instead of rediscovering it.

---

## 1. The dominant failure mode: measurements that measure nothing

Four times in two days a check came back clean, and the cleanliness was the bug. This is
now the single most likely thing to go wrong in this codebase's analysis work.

| # | the "result" | what was actually happening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | region census: 99.6–100% "surrounded" everywhere, incl. a **pants mesh at 100% breast poke** | `rays_hit` returns True=HIT; the script did `~rays_hit`, counting rays that ESCAPED — the metric inverted |
| 2 | partition audit: "**0 shapes over the bone cap**", whole pack clean | pynifly's `partition.bones` returns EMPTY, so `max(counts)=0 <= 78` and every shape passed vacuously |
| 3 | reskin A/B: 13 rows, **every number identical** between arms | the pass never ran — flag flip changed nothing, SHA1 byte-identical |
| 4 | thigh ranking: 21 armours "losing 47–96% of coverage" | the harness can't model those garments at all (§3); the loss is artifact |

**What caught each one was never the internal reasoning.** It was a sanity check against
a known answer: a pants mesh cannot cover a breast; a partition list cannot be empty; two
arms of an A/B cannot be byte-identical; a garment can't lose coverage from bones nothing
poses.

### The rules this produces

1. **Every run carries a NEGATIVE control** — an input that MUST score zero. Positive
   controls cannot catch an inversion; both arms move together. Two are pinned in the
   under-bust census (pants and underwear scored at the breast, must read 100% bare) and
   the run **aborts** if they fail.
2. **Assert the thing under test actually ran.** Before reporting any null result, diff
   the output (SHA1) or spy on the function. "No effect" and "never executed" are
   indistinguishable from the outside and have opposite implications.
3. **Check the check has data in it.** If a predicate reads a list, assert the list is
   non-empty. `max([]) == 0 <= threshold` passes every threshold ever written.
4. **State the resolution next to the number.** See §2.
5. **Filter shapes through `converted_garment_names()` before anything.** Counting the
   bundled source body as a garment turned "11 reachable" into 1. Phase 2 DROPS the
   source body; a body shape passing a gate is meaningless.

## 2. Numbers without a resolution are not numbers

`ray_exposure` is a hard boolean — the ray hits a triangle or it doesn't — so a body vert
sitting on the coverage boundary flips on hundredths of a unit. Measured: **jittering the
garment 0.02u flips 10–14 band verts**. A change under evaluation flipped 13 for a net of
+3, and was nearly recorded as a regression. It was the same size as the noise.

Shipped as a result (`scripts/analysis/mesh_penetration.py`):

* `exposure_with_margin(...)` — three-way exposed / covered / **AMBIGUOUS**, via a
  deterministic six-axis perturbation of the ray origin. `margin=0` reproduces
  `ray_exposure` exactly, asserted as a control.
* `noise_floor(...)` — jitter the garment, recount, report flipped/net per amplitude.

`scripts/analysis/underbust_census.py` now prints its resolution **before** any result. It also
surfaces a per-piece quality signal: one mesh reports **29 ambiguous verts against 28
exposed**, so nothing measured on it below ~14 verts means anything at all.

**Caveat learned the hard way:** a margin does NOT make a small delta vanish. On the
piece above the delta held at +2 across every margin — pure noise wanders in sign. The
margin tells you the *uncertainty band*; it does not adjudicate small effects.

## 3. The harness cannot see over half the pack

`scripts/analysis/multipose_clip_test.py` poses the SKELETON and applies linear blend skinning. It
does **not** simulate HDT-SMP. Cloth weighted to SMP chain bones is driven by no posed
bone, so the harness holds it still while the body swings out from under it, and reports
a near-total coverage loss that is entirely an artifact.

Of the 21 armours "losing >40% of thigh under crouch", the worst seven carry **0.0% of
their thigh weight on any bone the harness poses**. Pack-wide: **768 of 1424 armour-regions
(54%) are in this blind spot.**

**The gate to apply, every time:** compute the fraction of a region's garment weight
sitting on bones in `pose_set.POSE_SET`; trust the number only at **>=70% posed weight**.

Applying it collapses the defect list from **43 armours to 14**, and from 25 over 40% to
5. The real class is **long robes at the thigh under crouch** — properly weighted to body
bones, an ordinary skinning problem, not an SMP one.

## 4. The structural finding: skin passes cannot reach the worst clipping

Three independent investigations converged on one fact.

| investigation | worst-defect population | reachable by a skin pass |
|---|---|---|
| thigh under crouch | 21 armours | **1** |
| butt (SMP, no butt bones) | 35 armours | 14 |
| collider follow gap | hide cuirasses | 0 |

Every skin pass skips colliders, soft-body, layered cloth and HDT-SMP-rigged shapes — and
that is precisely where the worst defects live. Each exclusion is individually justified
and at least one is in-game-proven, so this is not a bug in any pass. It means
**deformation matching, the strongest measured lever, is permanently pointed at the
already-clean population.**

The full reskin gate — check ALL of it before calling anything reachable: body ref present,
shape has bones, not in `RESKIN_SKIP_NAMES`, not softbody, not collider, not
`_shape_has_fine_animation_bones`, not `_shape_is_head_dominant`, not layered, and **not
`_shape_has_hdt_smp_rigging`**. `CBBE2UBE_RESKIN_KEEP=1` clears only the morph-TRI
condition — not one of the others.

## 5. What was tried and died — do not repeat

| attempt | verdict |
|---|---|
| **Orientation-gated anti-poke** (suppress push where the body faces down-and-forward) | Overfit. better 4 / same 5 / WORSE 2, and the in-game-confirmed piece scored 14→14 with its sibling 10→12. Its own tuning piece supplied −21 of the −31 "net win". |
| **Unmask colliders for the reskin** | Zero benefit (22.7%→22.7%) AND it grafted physics breast-chain bones onto the bust collider — the known tear-off configuration. The exclusion is load-bearing. |
| **Normal-field divergence** as the under-bust mechanism | Disproven: under-curve is the 57.9th percentile, *less* divergent than body average. |
| **Concavity** as the under-bust mechanism | Disproven, in the opposite direction: the under-curve is one of the most CONVEX regions (11.9th percentile). |
| **`RESKIN_EXCLUDE_SCALE_BONES` as a live CTD hazard** | Non-issue. `False` is deliberate and documented ("keep False" — scale bones are the body-tracking layer); the CTD is handled by `_split_oversize_partition`. Audited: 12 over-cap shapes in 3,937 NIFs, all split to `{78, spill}`, zero over-cap partitions. |

## 6. What stands

* **The under-bust cause is real and confirmed:** `clear_armor_outside_body` — the
  anti-poke — causes **~4× the poke it prevents** at the under-bust (28→7 when disabled).
  It uncovers only 71 verts body-wide but 94% of them are at the bust. The *fixes* failed;
  the *diagnosis* holds.
* **Containment is the sound poke test** — a ray cone about the outward normal, not
  rim-distance (which structurally cannot return "poke" on a boundary-heavy garment) and
  not signed-distance-by-nearest-normal (a shell has two faces). Chest is 5–7× worse than
  any other region, matching the in-game report from an independent measurement.
* **`find_source` now resolves BSA-packed sources** (`f80a21a`). It scanned loose files
  only, so 24 of 308 census armours (8%) were invisible — including the three largest
  real pose defects. Now 284 loose + 24 BSA, **0 unresolved**.
* **Hide armour diagnosed**, see `../worklog/HIDE_ARMOR_ZERO_CLIP.md`. The garment is simultaneously
  the bust cover and the skirt's collider; the vanilla sibling `bandit/body1f` solves the
  same problem by giving the collider role to a **separate** shape, leaving its bust
  garment free to carry breast weights. That is why the 7c graft had to be reverted, and
  it is the basis of the split-collider build now under in-game test.

---

## 7. The one-line version

*A metric that reports "no problem" is indistinguishable from a metric that cannot see
the problem.* Every wasted hour of these two days traces to that sentence, and every
useful result came from a control that made the difference visible.
